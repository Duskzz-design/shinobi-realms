Shinobi Realms Privacy Policy

Shinobi Realms is a free browser game. When you sign in with Google we receive your Google account ID and email address. We use them only to sign you in and keep your ninja saved.

We store your ninja's name, look, village, level, stats and in-game progress. Your ninja name, look, village and rank are shown to other players. Chat messages you send are shown to other players in real time and are not stored.

We do not sell your data or show third-party ads. Data is stored with our database provider (Supabase) and the site is hosted by Netlify.

To delete your account and all game data, email the address on the Google sign-in screen and we will remove it.
